About

A very small studio with a very long to-do list.

Radd Games makes puzzle games for phones: the kind you open for five minutes and close forty minutes later, slightly proud of yourself.

Who we are

Radd Games is an independent studio from India. The games are designed, built and published by Radd (Sriram Goparaju on paper), with a great deal of help from a small circle of patient friends who test everything first.

What we make

Calm, fair puzzle games. Easy to start, hard to put down, and never rude about it: no ads in the middle of a puzzle, no sign-up wall, and no dark tricks to keep you tapping.

How to reach us

Write to support@raddgames.com. For help with a particular game, its support page is the quickest route.

Things we promise

No ads in the middle of a puzzle.

Ads wait for a natural pause, like the end of a level. And every game lets you pay once to switch them off.

No sign-up. Not even a tiny one.

No email, no password, no profile picture. Open the game and play.

Your data, your call.

Every game says plainly what it collects and gives you a way to delete it. The links are at the bottom of every page.

A real person reads the inbox.

Stuck, confused, or found a bug wearing a tiny hat? Write to us. We usually reply within two working days.
