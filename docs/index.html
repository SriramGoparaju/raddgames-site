Inkbloom

A calm page of puzzles, every day.

Eight fresh puzzles every day, drawn from eleven logic games: nonogram, sudoku, word search and more. No timers shouting at you, and no ads while you solve.

  • 11logic games
  • 8new puzzles a day

For people who solve to think. And to avoid the dishes.

Lumiwire

Join every pair. Cross nothing. Fill the board.

A calm circuit puzzle. Drag a wire from each socket to its twin, never cross another wire, and cover every cell. Every board has exactly one answer, so careful thinking always gets you there.

  • 400levels in eight chapters
  • 1answer for every board
  • 60seconds in a Blitz round

Like untangling your earphones, except this time you win.

Shapelings

Every piece fits. You just have to find where.

A relaxing block jigsaw. Drag candy-coloured pieces into a shape until it is full. Every puzzle has an answer, and you never have to rush.

  • 300levels in ten worlds
  • 3grids: squares, triangles, hexagons
  • 1Daily puzzle, same for everyone

Tetris's calmer cousin, who brought snacks.

Loopo

Draw a loop. Pop the bubbles.

Bubbles drift across a sunny lagoon. Circle a group of one colour with your finger and they pop. Loop bigger groups to earn bombs, darts and colour-clearing powers.

  • 1finger is all you need
  • 3powers: Bomb, Colour, Dart
  • 2ways to play: Journey and Survival

Lasso practice for people who don't own a horse.

House rules

Things we promise. Even for games that don't exist yet.

No ads in the middle of a puzzle.

Ads wait for a natural pause, like the end of a level. And every game lets you pay once to switch them off.

No sign-up. Not even a tiny one.

No email, no password, no profile picture. Open the game and play.

Your data, your call.

Every game says plainly what it collects and gives you a way to delete it. The links are at the bottom of every page.

A real person reads the inbox.

Stuck, confused, or found a bug wearing a tiny hat? Write to us. We usually reply within two working days.

Play before everyone else.

All four games are in closed testing on Android right now. Want in? Send us the Gmail address you use on Google Play and we'll add you to the list. We use it for that and nothing else.
