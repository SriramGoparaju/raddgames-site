How to play Loopo

The rules

  1. Bubbles drift across the lagoon. Draw a loop around bubbles of one colour and lift your finger: they pop.
  2. One colour per loop. If another colour is inside, nothing pops. There's no penalty, so just try again.
  3. A loop doesn't have to be neat. Finish close to where you started and it closes itself.
  4. Bigger loops score more, and a big one leaves a power behind.
  5. Never loop an urchin, the spiky one with the frown. It ends your run.

The three powers

Bomb. Pops everything around it. Loop it where it sits, or drop one from your pocket.

Colour. Loop it together with one colour and every bubble of that colour pops, wherever it is.

Dart. Flies off and bounces around for three seconds, popping every plain bubble it touches.

Powers you've saved live in your pocket. Double-tap the water, or press pause, to use one. You can use up to three in a run.

Also in the water

Urchin. Draw around it, never over it. Looping one ends the run.

Gold. A coin bubble. Catch it in any loop and it pays out.

Key. Carries on a Survival run that has just ended, with your score kept.

Stars. Every Journey level gives up to three, for the goal and for your score.

Two ways to play

Journey

Levels on a map, grouped into ponds. Each level has one goal and a timer. A failed level costs a heart, a win costs nothing, and hearts come back with time.

Survival

No timer. Bubbles keep coming and things speed up. If 30 bubbles crowd the lagoon for three seconds, the run is over. Your best score of the week goes on the Ranks board.
