Loopo

Bubbles drift across a sunny lagoon. Circle a group of one colour with your finger and they pop. Loop bigger groups to earn bombs, darts and colour-clearing powers.

Why you might like it

One finger, one rule

Draw a loop around bubbles of one colour. That's the whole game, and it's surprisingly hard to stop.

Loops don't have to be neat

End your line near where it started and the loop closes itself. Wobbly circles welcome.

Big loops earn powers

Catch a big group and it leaves a Bomb, a Dart or a Colour bubble behind. Save them, or set them off at once.

A Journey to follow

Levels on a map, each with one simple goal shown as a picture. Hearts refill while you're away.

Survival for one more go

No timer and no levels. Just keep the lagoon from filling up, and chase this week's best score.

Ads when you choose

Videos for a reward only play when you ask for one. A short ad may show between rounds, and one purchase removes those.

How it plays

  1. Bubbles drift across the lagoon. Draw a loop around bubbles of one colour and lift your finger: they pop.
  2. One colour per loop. If another colour is inside, nothing pops. There's no penalty, so just try again.
  3. A loop doesn't have to be neat. Finish close to where you started and it closes itself.
