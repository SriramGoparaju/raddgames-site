How to play Lumiwire

Join every pair. Cross nothing. Fill the board.

The rules

  1. Each pair of sockets has its own colour and its own symbol. Drag from one socket to its twin to lay a wire between them.
  2. Wires run along the grid, never diagonally. They never cross each other and never pass through another pair's socket.
  3. Drag through another wire to cut it: the cut part springs back. Drag from the middle of one of your wires to re-route it from there.
  4. The board is complete when every pair is joined and every cell is covered.
  5. Undo, Redo and Clear sit under the board. There are no wrong moves, no lives and no time limit in the Journey.

Stars

  • 3 stars. Every wire drawn in one stroke, with no wire revealed by a hint.
  • 2 stars. Finished any other way: a wire took two strokes, was cut or re-routed, or was revealed by a hint.

Three ways to play

Journey

The main road: 400 levels in eight chapters. Levels climb gently, every sixth one is a Challenge, and each chapter ends with a boss.

Daily

One board a day for everyone, new at 00:00 UTC, using only obstacles you have already met. The timer stays hidden unless you switch it on; your time goes to the Daily leaderboard. Past days wait in the calendar.

Blitz

Opens after Journey level 10. Solve as many tiny boards as you can in 60 seconds: 4×4, then 5×5, then 6×6. A board drawn in one stroke per wire adds two seconds. Everyone gets the same boards each day.

The circuit tricks

Each chapter adds one new idea, introduced on an easy board with a one-line tip.

TrickArrivesHow it works
Dead chipsLevel 51Dark blocks that are not part of the board. Wires go around them, and they cut boards into new shapes.
Cut tracesLevel 101A broken-trace mark on the edge between two cells. No wire may cross that edge, but both cells still need filling.
FusesLevel 151A fused socket starts dark and shows the symbol of the pair that powers it. Connect that pair and the fuse blows: the socket lights up in its own colour, ready to be wired.
RelaysLevel 201A diamond pad ringed in one colour. Only that colour's wire may pass through it, and that wire must.
Soldered tracesLevel 251A few cells of a wire already laid, with a joint at each end. They can't be cut or cleared, and their pair's wire has to run through them.
BridgesLevel 301An overpass that takes two wires straight through: one passes under, the other crosses on top.
ViasLevel 351Two holes with the same letter. A wire that goes into one comes out of the other, so far-apart parts of the board join up.
Wrap-around edgesLevel 371On a board with dashed side edges, a wire leaving the right edge comes back in on the left, and the other way round.
