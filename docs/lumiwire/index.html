Wire puzzle

Lumiwire

Join every pair. Cross nothing. Fill the board.

A calm circuit puzzle. Drag a wire from each socket to its twin, never cross another wire, and cover every cell. Every board has exactly one answer, so careful thinking always gets you there.

Why you might like it

One answer, always

Every board is proven by a solver to have exactly one solution, with every obstacle in place. Nothing is hidden and nothing is random, so careful thinking always gets you there.

Eight chapters, eight new ideas

Dead chips, cut traces, fuses, relays, soldered traces, bridges, vias and wrap-around edges, each introduced gently on an easy board with a one-line tip.

Stars for style, not speed

Draw every wire in one stroke for three stars. In the Journey the clock never counts.

A Daily that doesn't nag

One board a day, the same for everyone, with a leaderboard if you want it. No streak counter on the home screen, and nothing lost for missing a day.

Blitz when you have a minute

Sixty seconds of tiny boards. A board drawn in one stroke per wire adds two seconds. Daily and weekly leaderboards for the competitive.

Easy on the eyes

Soft greys and gold, in dark and in light. Every pair has a symbol as well as a colour, and the colours are tuned to stay distinct for colour-blind players.

Plays offline

Everything plays without a connection. Your results go up to the leaderboards when you're back online.

Ads that wait their turn

Never in the middle of a board: a short Ad break every third level from level 6, and Remove Ads, once, to end them. Other ads play only when you ask for one.

How it plays

  1. Each pair of sockets has its own colour and its own symbol. Drag from one socket to its twin to lay a wire between them.
  2. Wires run along the grid, never diagonally. They never cross each other and never pass through another pair's socket.
  3. Drag through another wire to cut it: the cut part springs back. Drag from the middle of one of your wires to re-route it from there.
