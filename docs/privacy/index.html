This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads. Nothing here tracks you or builds a profile.
  • Nothing loaded from other companies. The fonts, pictures and scripts are served from this site itself.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and its domain is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, these services process technical details such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep them secure.
  • Email. If you write to us, we use your address and your message only to answer you. Ask us to delete the conversation and we will.
  • Testers. If you ask to test a game, we add the Google account address you send us to that game's tester list on Google Play, and use it for nothing else. Ask to be removed at any time.
