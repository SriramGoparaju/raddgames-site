Shapelings Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Shapelings is a block jigsaw puzzle game. This policy explains what it collects, why, who handles it, and what you can do about it.

  • There is no sign-up and no account. You never give us your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your progress, hints, gems, block styles and settings are saved on your phone only. We never receive them.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID. Where the law requires a consent form, no ad is requested until you have answered it.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports are switched off when you install the app, and switched on only after that consent step.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not share it with data brokers.

Who is responsible

Shapelings is published by Sriram Goparaju, in India. For any question about this policy or your data, write to support@raddgames.com.

What stays on your phone

Shapelings saves your game on your phone: the levels you finished or skipped, the Daily puzzles you solved, the puzzle you are in the middle of, your hints and gems, the block styles you own and use, your settings (sound, vibration), how many levels have passed since the last ad, and whether you own Remove ads. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, and there is no cloud copy. It stays until you reset your progress in Settings, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

What is collected, and why

How the game is played

Firebase Analytics, a Google service, counts how Shapelings is played, so we can see which levels are too hard and where players stop. The app records: that the tutorial was finished; that a level was started, finished or skipped (with the world and level, whether it was a Hard level, the seconds taken, the moves, undos and hints used); that a hint was used or gained, or you ran out of hints; that gems were gained or spent and on what; that an ad was shown or could not be shown; that a purchase was started, finished or failed, and which item; that you changed block style; that a Daily puzzle was started or finished; and that a world was finished. Analytics also records basic information about the device (model, operating system, app version, language, and a country estimated from the IP address) under its own random identifier for this installation of the app.

Analytics never collects your device's advertising ID, and it is not used for advertising. Collection is switched off when the app is installed. It is switched on only after the consent step described under Advertising: where Google's consent form applies, only if you allow storage and measurement on the device; elsewhere, once the app has confirmed that no form is needed where you are.

Crash reports

Firebase Crashlytics records crashes, and some errors the app recovers from, so they can be found and fixed. A report holds the device model, the operating system and app version, the state of the app and the place in the code that failed, and a random identifier for this installation of the app. Crash reporting follows the same consent step as Analytics: it is off until that step allows it.

Settings we can change remotely

The app asks Firebase Remote Config for numbers that can change without an app update, such as how many hints a new player starts with or how far apart ads are. The request carries a random identifier for this installation of the app, the app version, and your device's language and region settings. Nothing about your play is sent with it.

Checking that requests come from the real app

Firebase App Check asks Google Play Integrity (on Android) or Apple's App Attest (on iPhone and iPad) to confirm that requests to our Firebase services come from a genuine copy of Shapelings on a genuine device. This protects the services from abuse. It is not used to identify you or for advertising.

Advertising

Shapelings shows ads through Google AdMob. There are two kinds: a short full-screen ad between levels (at most after every third finished level, never in your first five levels, at least a minute apart, and never in the middle of a puzzle), and rewarded videos that play only when you choose to watch one, for a hint when you have run out. There are no banner ads. Remove ads, a one-time purchase, ends the ads between levels; the videos you choose stay available.

When it shows an ad, Google's advertising software collects, by Google's own description, your device's advertising ID, its IP address (which may be used to estimate your general location), information about the device and the app, and how you interact with ads, such as taps and video views. Google uses this to choose, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, under Google's privacy policy. Shapelings asks AdMob only for ads suitable for a general audience with parental guidance, and tells it that the app is not directed at children.

Asking you first

Where the law gives you a choice (the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, and the US states with their own privacy laws), Google's consent or opt-out form appears when you first open the app, before any ad is requested; nothing is fetched from AdMob until you have answered. If you decline, Google may still show limited or non-personalised ads, within the choices you made. Where the form applies, Settings shows Privacy options, which reopens it, so you can change your answer at any time; if your new answer no longer allows ads, the app stops requesting them.

On iPhone and iPad, Apple's question about whether the app may track you across other companies' apps and websites comes once, after the consent form. Saying no changes only how relevant the ads are; the game is exactly the same. You can change the answer in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's privacy settings.

Purchases

Hint packs, gem packs and Remove ads are bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store, which handle the payment; we never see or store your card or bank details. RevenueCat, a service that manages in-app purchases, receives the store's receipt for each purchase together with an anonymous identifier for your copy of the app, so it can confirm what you bought and let you restore Remove ads. Your phone remembers which purchases have already been credited, so that nothing is credited twice.

Rating the game

After you finish a world, the app may ask Google Play or the App Store, once, to show their own rating prompt. The store decides whether it appears and handles anything you write; we receive nothing from it.

What we don't collect

Shapelings does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or messages, and it does not ask for your location. It has no accounts, no chat, no leaderboards and no way to contact other players. Google's ad and analytics services may estimate a general location from your IP address, as described above.

Who else processes your data

We use these services, and nothing else:

Google Firebase: Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, App CheckUsage events and crash reports (after consent), remote settings, checks that requests come from the real app
Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging PlatformAds, and the consent form where the law requires one

Each of these processes data on our behalf under its own terms. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts; both receive your IP address when you visit, under their own policies. The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

How long it is kept

  • On your phone: until you reset your progress, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Crash reports: 90 days, on Crashlytics' own schedule.
  • Analytics events: on Google's schedule, at most fourteen months.
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play, the App Store and RevenueCat for their accounting and tax records.

Everything the app sends travels encrypted.

Your choices

  • Answer the consent form as you wish, and change your mind with Privacy options in Settings, where the form applies.
  • Say no to tracking. On iPhone and iPad, in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. The game works exactly the same either way.
  • Delete your data. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, removes everything it kept on your phone. The delete-your-data page explains what others keep and how to ask about it.
  • Ask us. For a copy of your data, or anything else, write to support@raddgames.com. Because there are no accounts, the records held by Google and RevenueCat carry only random identifiers, and we may be unable to tell which ones are yours.

If you are in the UK, the EU or another region with similar law, you also have the right to access and correct your data, to have it deleted, to object to how we process it, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Shapelings is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, write to support@raddgames.com and we will remove what we can.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and say so in the app before the change takes effect.
