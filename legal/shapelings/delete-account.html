Delete your Shapelings data

Last updated {{UPDATED}}

Shapelings has no accounts: you never sign up, and nothing is stored under your name or email address. Your game lives on your phone, and you can remove it at any time.

To delete everything Shapelings keeps on your phone: uninstall the app, or open your phone's Settings → Apps → Shapelings → Storage → Clear data.

To start the game over but keep the app: in Shapelings, Settings → Reset progress. This puts the Journey back to its first level. Your Daily puzzles, hints, gems, styles and Remove ads stay.

What is deleted

  • Your progress: levels finished or skipped, Daily puzzles solved, and the puzzle you were in the middle of.
  • Your hints, gems and block styles, and your settings.
  • The app's record of your purchases and of ads shown. Remove ads belongs to your store account, so "Restore purchases" brings it back if you install the app again.

Hints and gems are deleted with the app's data and are not refunded.

What others keep, and why

  • Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics, only after consent) are recorded under a random identifier for one installation of the app, not under your name. Uninstalling the app ends that identifier; events already recorded expire on Google's schedule, at most fourteen months.
  • Crash reports (Crashlytics, only after consent) carry a random installation identifier and are deleted after 90 days.
  • Ads: Google AdMob's data is tied to your device's advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your phone's privacy settings. Google's own controls are at My Ad Center.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play or the App Store, and by RevenueCat on our behalf, for their accounting and tax records. They carry only an anonymous identifier.

Asking us

We hold nothing that tells us who you are, so we cannot look you up. If you want us to try to remove records held for us, email {{EMAIL}} with "Delete my Shapelings data" in the subject line and roughly when you played. We reply within 30 days, and we will say plainly if nothing can be matched to you.
